Shelf — Turn your idea
into a physical product

Shelf is an AI-guided design tool that walks creators through the early-stage soft goods manufacturing process and generates factory-ready assets — including a tech pack and SVG pattern file. It removes the time, cost, and confusion that stops amateur designers from getting physical products made.

Research · Competitive landscape
Research · Customer interviews
Interview 01 · Open Threads
Program Manager, Open Threads
Top need
Pattern generation — the ability to produce a usable pattern from a design concept was the single most requested feature.
Workflow today
Designers start with competitor examples and open-ended inspiration prompts. The path from idea to factory-ready file is fragmented and manual.
Key insight
Design input should remain open and prompt-driven — the tool shouldn't over-constrain the creative stage.
Recommended connections
The Factory Buffalo, Sew Valley, ISAIC, and technical designers focused on streamlining production timelines.
Interview 02 · ISAIC Detroit
Head of Innovation, ISAIC Detroit
Gap confirmed
The jump from idea to finished product is one of the industry's biggest pain points — designers are underprepared, and manufacturers can't engage until minimum documentation requirements are met.
Pivot insight
The most valuable version of Shelf supports the technical designer — reducing hours on tech pack creation, digital patterns, and material sourcing — rather than going directly to the end customer first.
Technical requirement
Focus on agnostic DXF files compatible across Gerber, Pathfinder, and other systems. AI-generated BOMs are immediately identifiable as non-technical work by experienced manufacturers.
Market opportunity
The US economy runs on small brands. Larger companies already have overseas supply chains. Shelf's opening is with small entrepreneurs who need a path from ideation to domestic production.

Invoice Generator

Paste into Claude with Notion connected to generate a client invoice from your time entries.

You are a billing assistant. I need you to create a client invoice. 1. Search my Notion for time entries for [CLIENT NAME] from [START DATE] to [END DATE]. 2. For each entry, pull the date, task description, and hours worked. 3. Calculate each line item: hours x [HOURLY RATE]. 4. Write outcome-based descriptions (e.g. "Strategy session and kickoff" not just "call"). 5. Generate a professional invoice as a Word document (.docx) with: - FROM: [YOUR NAME / COMPANY] - BILL TO: [CLIENT NAME] - Invoice number, date, due date (15 days out) - Line items table: Date | Description | Hours | Rate | Amount - Subtotal and Total Due rows - Payment instructions section 6. Save and share the file. Replace all [BRACKETS] with your own details before running.
Platform Strategy Builder

Paste into Claude with your business idea — builds a 9-element framework first, then expands into a full strategy doc.

I want to build a platform strategy for [YOUR BUSINESS IDEA]. STAGE 1 - Generate a 9-element pitch framework: 1. CLEAR PROBLEM - What broken reality does this solve? 2. UNIQUE SOLUTION - What does this product do? Who is it built by and for? 3. TARGET MARKET - Who are the customers? Include TAM, revenue size, growth signal. 4. HOW YOU MAKE MONEY - Revenue model with a 3-year arc (Year to Milestone to $ARR). 5. TRACTION - What proof points, prior work, or relationships exist? 6. GO-TO-MARKET - Phase 1 / Phase 2 / Phase 3. Start with network before scaling. 7. TEAM - Who is behind this and why are they uniquely qualified? 8. IMPACT AND VISION - What does the world look like when this wins? 9. CTA - What is the specific ask right now? Keep each section to 2-5 punchy sentences. End by asking what to sharpen. STAGE 2 - Only after I approve, expand into a full platform strategy doc. Tone: confident, mission-aware, data-forward, never hedging. My idea: [DESCRIBE YOUR BUSINESS IDEA IN 2-3 SENTENCES]
