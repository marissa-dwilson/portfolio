Invoice Generator

Paste this prompt into Claude (with Notion connected) to generate a client invoice from your time entries.

You are a billing assistant. I need you to create a client invoice. 1. Search my Notion for time entries for [CLIENT NAME] from [START DATE] to [END DATE]. 2. For each entry, pull the date, task description, and hours worked. 3. Calculate each line item: hours × [HOURLY RATE]. 4. Write outcome-based descriptions (e.g. "Strategy session & kickoff" not just "call"). 5. Generate a professional invoice as a Word document (.docx) with: - FROM: [YOUR NAME / COMPANY] - BILL TO: [CLIENT NAME] - Invoice number, date, due date (15 days out) - Line items table: Date | Description | Hours | Rate | Amount - Subtotal and Total Due rows - Payment instructions section 6. Save and share the file. Replace all [BRACKETS] with your own details before running.
Platform Strategy Builder

Paste this into Claude with your business idea and it will build a 9-element pitch framework, then expand it into a full strategy document.

I want to build a platform strategy for [YOUR BUSINESS IDEA]. STAGE 1 — Generate a 9-element pitch framework: 1. CLEAR PROBLEM — What broken reality does this solve? 2. UNIQUE SOLUTION — What does this product do? Who is it built by and for? 3. TARGET MARKET — Who are the customers? Include TAM, revenue size, growth signal. 4. HOW YOU MAKE MONEY — Revenue model with a 3-year arc (Year → Milestone → $ARR). 5. TRACTION — What proof points, prior work, or relationships exist? 6. GO-TO-MARKET — Phase 1 / Phase 2 / Phase 3. Start with network before scaling. 7. TEAM — Who is behind this and why are they uniquely qualified? 8. IMPACT & VISION — What does the world look like when this wins? 9. CTA — What is the specific ask right now? Keep each section to 2–5 punchy sentences. End by asking what to sharpen. STAGE 2 — Only after I approve, expand into a full platform strategy doc. Tone: confident, mission-aware, data-forward, never hedging. My idea: [DESCRIBE YOUR BUSINESS IDEA IN 2–3 SENTENCES]
